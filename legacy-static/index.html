01 — about

Who I am

I'm a wireless systems researcher with 7+ years building and testing 4G/5G/6G networks — from open-source testbeds (OAI, srsRAN, Open5GS, free5GC) running on real SDR hardware, to the physical-layer math underneath (OFDM, Massive MIMO, precoding). My PhD introduced Ramanujan Periodic Subspace Division Multiplexing (RPSDM), a new waveform inspired by number theory that outperforms OFDM on PAPR and BER.

Currently I work on Terrestrial-to-Non-Terrestrial handover and Doppler compensation at the University of Luxembourg. I like problems that sit at the intersection of theory and hardware — where an idea from a math paper has to survive contact with a real USRP.
